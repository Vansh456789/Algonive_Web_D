🚀 Getting Started

For TaskFlow (Personal Task Manager):

  1. Click "Open TaskFlow" above or go to Task_1/index.html
  2. Start creating tasks with title, description, and due date
  3. Use filters and sorting to organize your work
  4. Tasks are automatically saved to your browser's storage

For CollabTask (Team Management):

  1. Click "Open CollabTask" above or go to Task_2/index.html
  2. Login with demo account (demo@email.com / password123)
  3. Or create a new account with your team name
  4. Create tasks and assign them to team members
  5. Track progress with status updates and filters
